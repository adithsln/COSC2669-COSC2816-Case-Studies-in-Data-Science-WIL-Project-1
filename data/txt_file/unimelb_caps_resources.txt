Title: University of Melbourne - Counselling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
Content: The Counselling and Psychological Services (CAPS) at the University of Melbourne provides enrolled students with free, confidential psychological support delivered by experienced mental health practitioners. Services include short-term individual counselling (available in-person and online), group counselling to connect with peers, and wellbeing workshops. CAPS also offer extensive self-help resources and tip sheets covering common mental health issues such as anxiety and depression, internet overuse and addiction, and stress management. Students struggling with digital habits can access guided exercises, including breath training, meaningfulness motivation and sleep exercises with accompanying audio files. For immediate crisis, students cam access urgent mental health support 24/7 through the University's dedicated Mental Health Crisis Support Services.
