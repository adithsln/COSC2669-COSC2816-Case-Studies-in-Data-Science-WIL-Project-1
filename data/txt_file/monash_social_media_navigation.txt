Title: Monash University - Navigating Social Media and Mental Health
Content: High use of social media and technology can negatively impact youth self-esteem with two in three young people feeling pressure to look good online. To manage social media use, students should promote healthy technology habits. Research shows using technology at night use can negatively impact sleep quality. It is recommended to stop using technology for about 30 minutes toa an hour before bedtime and to keep devices in living spaces rather than the bedroom. If cyberbullying occurs-such as attempts to intimidate, exclude, or humiliate others through hurtful messages or images-students should not try to retaliate. Instead, block the bully, repot the behavior to the social media platform's safety center, and speak to a trusted adult. Monash students can seek advice and support from the Monash Sager Community Unit, the Monash Counselling Service, or call the 24/7 Benestar phone counselling service on 1300 788 336.
