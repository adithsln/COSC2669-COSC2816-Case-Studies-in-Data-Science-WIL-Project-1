Title: RMIT Student Wellbeing and Mental Health Support
Content: RMIT University provides comprehensive support for students experiencing mental health challenges, including anxiety, depression, and stress. If you are an RMIT UP student who require counselling support, contact RMIT UP Student Wellbeing at wellbeing@rmit.edu.au or call 03 9657 5800. For RMIT university students, the RMIT Counselling and Psychological Service offer free and confidential short-term counselling. If you require urgent assistance outside the business hours, contact RMIT Urgent mental health support at 1300 305 737 or text 0488 884 162. Counselling gives you the opportunity to learn different ways to manage your mental health, find your emotional balance, and receive referrals to external specialists if needed. Helpful external services include Lifeline (13 11 14), Beyondblue (1300 22 46 36) and Headspace, which provides free online and telephone support to young people aged 12 to 25. All counselling sessions are strictly confidential unless there is a perceived risk of safety and legal requirement.
